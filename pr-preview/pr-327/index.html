Concepts

Every tenant in its own space

Each team or tenant gets their own isolated workspace, built on kcp, a multi-tenant control plane for Kubernetes-style APIs. Add shards and regions as you grow.

One API model for everything

Every service is managed the same way declaratively with Kubernetes Resource Model (KRM). One common language shared between providers and consumers.

Plug in any provider

No more custom glue code. Providers expose their services through their own control plane, and consumers bind to them through theirs.

AI agents act as you

Turn on the optional MCP integration and connect your AI agent to Platform Mesh. It works with your own sign-in, limited to what your permissions allow.

Fits around what you already run

Your current clusters and services stay where they are. Bring them into the mesh, or build something new on top.

Governed in the open

Platform Mesh lives under NeoNephos and the Linux Foundation Europe. All code, roadmap, decisions are public and no single company can change the terms on you.
